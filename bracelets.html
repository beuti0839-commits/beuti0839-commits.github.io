How to build an easy stack

Start with one fine chain, add one slightly thicker piece, then one bangle or your watch. Keep to one metal tone, and leave the other wrist bare or with a single piece so the look stays light.

Wearing a bracelet with a watch

Put the watch first and one or two thin bracelets next to it. Thin chains sit nicely beside a leather or metal strap without scratching the watch face.

Sizing and care

Check the length in the listing and look for an extender chain or an adjustable closure. Take bracelets off before washing hands with soap, swimming or using lotion, and keep them in a soft pouch so they stay shiny longer.
