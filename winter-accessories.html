Winter accessories that keep you warm and elegant

Winter · A 4-minute read

Cold weather is no reason to give up on looking put together. A few well-chosen winter accessories in warm neutral tones make any coat look elegant, and keep you comfortable.

1. A soft knit beanie

A ribbed beanie in camel, cream or chocolate brown is warm and flattering. Look for a soft, non-itchy knit and a fold-over cuff you can adjust.

2. Touchscreen gloves

Leather-look or knit gloves with touchscreen fingertips let you use your phone without taking them off. A slim fit looks the most elegant.

3. An oversized scarf

A large, soft scarf works as a scarf, a wrap or a blanket on flights. Warm camel goes with almost every coat.

4. Ear warmers

A knit or faux-fur headband keeps your ears warm without flattening your hair. Great for short walks and school runs.

5. Cozy socks for home

Soft knit socks in neutral tones are a small comfort for cold evenings, and a lovely small gift too.

Shop cozy knit socks on Amazon

How to make winter accessories look expensive

Choose two or three colors and repeat them: for example a camel scarf, brown gloves and a cream beanie. Keep knits de-pilled and stored folded, not hung.
