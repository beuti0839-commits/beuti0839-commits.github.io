The sunglasses shapes that suit every face

Sunglasses · A 4-minute read

The right pair of sunglasses can make a simple outfit look finished. You don't need to spend much. You only need a frame that balances your face and lenses that protect your eyes.

1. Find your face shape

Pull your hair back and look straight into a mirror. Notice which part is widest (forehead, cheekbones or jaw) and whether your face is longer than it is wide. The aim is contrast: angular frames soften round faces, and rounded frames soften angular ones.

Oval

Balanced and slightly longer than wide. Almost any shape works, so try square, cat-eye or oversized frames.

Round

Soft curves and a similar length and width. Choose square, rectangular or wayfarer-style frames to add definition.

Square

A strong jaw and a wide forehead. Choose round, oval or aviator frames to soften the angles.

Heart

A wider forehead and a narrow chin. Choose aviators, round frames or light rimless styles, which keep the top of the face from looking heavy.

Long

Noticeably longer than wide. Choose tall, oversized frames to shorten the face visually.

2. Get the size right

  • The frame should be about as wide as your face at the temples, with no sharp edges sticking out past it.
  • Your eyes should sit near the centre of each lens.
  • Your eyebrows should show just above the top of the frame.
  • When you smile, the frames shouldn't lift off your nose.

3. Pick a colour that's easy to wear

Black and tortoiseshell go with almost everything, so either is a safe first pair. Gold metal frames look elegant with warm neutrals, and silver suits cooler colours. Brown and grey lenses are the most comfortable for everyday wear.

4. Check before you buy

  • UV protection: look for "UV400" or "100% UV protection" in the product description. Dark lenses alone don't protect your eyes.
  • Polarised lenses (optional): they cut glare from water, roads and car windows. They're useful for driving and the beach.
  • Hinges: they should open and close smoothly, without wobbling.
  • Reviews and returns: read a few reviews for comments on fit, and make sure the store's return policy is easy to understand.

5. Keep them looking new

  • Clean the lenses with a microfibre cloth, not your shirt or a tissue.
  • Store them in a case and never lens-down.
  • Don't leave them on the car dashboard, because heat can warp the frames.
  • Put them on and take them off with both hands so the arms don't stretch.
