Elegant hair accessories that finish any look

Hair accessories · A 3-minute read

The right hair accessory turns a quick hairstyle into a finished look in seconds. These are the simple, elegant pieces worth having, plus how to choose them.

1. The claw clip

A medium claw clip holds a twisted updo in one move. Matte and tortoiseshell finishes look more expensive than shiny plastic. Choose a size that fits the thickness of your hair.

Shop claw clips on Amazon

2. Satin scrunchies

Satin and silk-feel scrunchies are gentle on hair and leave fewer dents than elastic bands. In brown, cream or black they look polished, even on your wrist.

Shop satin scrunchies on Amazon

3. A padded headband

A padded or knotted headband adds instant polish to straight hair or a low bun. Velvet works for winter; satin or leather-look for everyday.

Shop padded headbands on Amazon

4. Pearl and gold hair clips

Small pearl or gold-tone clips on one side look elegant for weddings, dinners and photos. Two or three are enough.

Shop pearl hair clips on Amazon

5. A hair scarf

A small satin scarf tied around a ponytail or as a headband adds color near your face. It doubles as a bag accent.

Shop hair scarves on Amazon

Keep them looking new

Store clips in a small box so the teeth do not break, wash satin scrunchies by hand, and choose one metal tone so your hair pieces match your jewelry.
