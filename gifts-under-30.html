Gift ideas for her under $30

Gifts · A 4-minute read

A good gift does not have to be expensive. It has to feel thoughtful and look a little luxurious. These ideas are small, elegant and easy to love, and most of them cost under $30. Check the current price on Amazon, since prices change often.

1. Gold hoop earrings

A pair of gold-tone hoops is the easiest jewelry gift: they go with everything and suit almost every style. Look for hypoallergenic, nickel-free posts.

How to make a small gift feel special

Wrap it in kraft paper with a brown or gold ribbon, add a short handwritten note, and if you can, pair two small things that match, like hoops and a necklace in the same gold tone.
