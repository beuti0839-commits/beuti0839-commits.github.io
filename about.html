About Beauty & You

Beauty & You is a fashion and accessories guide for women who love to look elegant without overspending. We write simple, practical guides on bags, jewelry, scarves and more, so you can choose pieces with confidence.

What we do

We research styles and share what to look for: the shape, the material, the details. We recommend products from online stores. We do not sell anything ourselves, so we do not hold stock, take orders or ship parcels. When you click a link to a store, you buy from that store, and the store handles your order, delivery and returns.

How this site earns money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes the price you pay. We only recommend styles and products we believe are useful to our readers.
