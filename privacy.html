Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit Beauty & You.

What we collect

This site does not ask you to create an account, and it has no forms, comments or newsletter. We do not collect your name, email address or other personal details through the site.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When you visit, these services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as they do for any website. Please read their own privacy policies for details.

Affiliate links and cookies

Some links on this site lead to online stores through affiliate networks. When you click such a link, the store or network may place a cookie in your browser to record that you came from our site, so that we can receive a commission if you buy. We do not control those cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Third-party websites

Stores we link to have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.
