How to style a scarf in 4 ways

Scarves · A 3-minute read

A scarf is the quickest way to make a plain outfit look finished. One square scarf can work as a neck accessory, a hair accessory and a bag accessory. Here are four simple ways to wear it, and none needs any practice.

1. The classic neck knot

Fold the scarf into a long, narrow strip. Wrap it once around your neck, then tie a small knot at the front or slightly to the side. It adds color near your face and works with a shirt, a knit or a blazer.

2. The bag accent

Tie the scarf around the handle of your bag, leaving the ends loose. This refreshes a bag you wear every day, and it is the easiest way to add a pop of color without changing your outfit.

3. The hair wrap

Fold the scarf into a strip and tie it around a ponytail or a low bun. Or wear it as a headband, with the knot at the nape of your neck or on top. It keeps hair off your face and looks put together.

4. The belt loop

Thread a long scarf through the loops of your trousers or jeans and tie it at the side. It gives a plain outfit some shape and personality.

Quick checklist before you buy: a size around 70 to 90 cm square is the most flexible, a fabric that holds a knot without slipping, and a color that works with at least three things you already own.

How to choose a color

Pick a shade that repeats a color in your wardrobe, or one that sits next to your skin tone and brightens your face. Neutral scarves work with everything. A bold print works best when the rest of your outfit stays simple.
