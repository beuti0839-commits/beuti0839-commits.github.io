Gold necklaces for every day: chains, pendants and layers

Jewelry · A 3-minute read

A fine gold necklace is the easiest way to make a plain T-shirt, a knit or a work blouse look finished. You only need a few pieces in warm gold tones to cover every day of the week.

5. A chunkier statement chain

How to layer without tangles

Use two or three lengths with about 5 cm (2 inches) between them, and mix one plain chain with one pendant. A necklace spacer clasp keeps them apart.

Shop necklace spacer clasps on Amazon

Match the length to your neckline

Short chains suit crew necks, a pendant that points down suits a V-neck, and a longer chain looks great over a high-neck knit. With a collared shirt, a short chain under the collar is enough.
